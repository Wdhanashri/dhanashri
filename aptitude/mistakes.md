## Re-test protocol

1. **Don't read the row.** Just the question. **Timer on.**
2. Correct **and fast** → tick. Two ticks → retires.
3. Slow again → the concept drops to **🔴** in `ledger.md`.

## What your dominant tag means

| Tag | The fix — **not** "do more questions" |
|---|---|
| `formula-blank` | Genuine gap. Back to the anchor, then `cheatsheet.md` daily until instant. |
| `slow-arith` | Not a knowledge problem. **Drill the fraction table and squares 1–30**, five minutes a day. |
| `misread` | Underline what's actually being asked before you compute. Costs 3 seconds, saves the mark. |
| `should-skip` | **The most valuable tag in this file.** You have a discipline problem, not a maths problem — practise the 90-second rule, not the topic. |
| `careless` | Slow down for 5 seconds before you commit an answer. More study won't touch this. |
| `no-shortcut` | You solved it the long way. Reread the shortcut and redo the question *with it*. |

**Expect `slow-arith` and `should-skip` to dominate early.** Neither is fixed by studying more — one is drilled, the other is a decision you practise making.
