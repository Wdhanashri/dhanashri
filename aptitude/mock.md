## ① A-18 · Time budget & skip discipline

**Anchor:** *"The question you skip is the reason you finish."*

**This is the concept that converts your knowledge into a score.** People with identical knowledge score 30 marks apart on this alone.

```
25 questions · 40 minutes  →  96 seconds per question, average
```

But you should never *aim* for the average. Aim for this shape:

```
PASS 1  (20 min)   →  the obvious ones. Answer, move. No question over 60s.
                      Expect to clear 12-15.
PASS 2  (15 min)   →  the ones you skipped that now look doable.
PASS 3  (5 min)    →  fill in EVERY remaining blank with a guess.
```

### The 90-second rule

> **If you haven't found the method within 90 seconds, mark it and move.**

Not "if you can't solve it" — **if you don't know the method.** Once you know the method, finish. If you don't, no amount of staring produces it, and you're spending three easy questions to buy one hard one.

```
4 minutes on one hard question   =   3 easy questions unattempted
                                     net: −2 marks, and you feel rushed after
```

### The rules that recover marks

| Rule | Why |
|---|---|
| **Never leave a blank** | Most OAs have no negative marking. Blank = wrong. Guess costs nothing. |
| **Eliminate before guessing** | Two out is a 50% shot. That's worth real marks over 25 questions. |
| **Do DI sets as a block** | One chart understood = 4–5 fast questions. Don't abandon after Q1. |
| **Attack your strongest section first** | Confidence early is worth actual accuracy later. |
| **Watch sectional cut-offs** | Many companies require you to clear **each** section. A perfect quant score won't rescue a failed verbal. |

**The Gotcha:** the most expensive mistake in an OA is **not a hard question — it's a medium question you refuse to let go of.** Hard ones you skip immediately. It's the "I'm nearly there" ones that eat four minutes.

**Recall:** *What's the 90-second rule, and what exactly are you deciding at 90 seconds?*

## Scoring the gate

| Result | Next |
|---|---|
| **9+/9+/4+ and zero blanks** | ✅ You're OA-ready. Move to weekly maintenance below. |
| **One section below target** | Redo only that part file's drills. Retest in 3 days. Everything else stays passed. |
| **Two or more below** | Two extra sessions on the 🔴 concepts + the Mistake Book. Not a setback — the system doing its job. |
| **Right answers but ran out of time** | **This is a strategy problem, not a knowledge one.** Redo the mock with the three-pass structure. Nothing to relearn. |

That last row matters. If you knew the answers and the clock beat you, don't restudy anything — **practise the passes.**
