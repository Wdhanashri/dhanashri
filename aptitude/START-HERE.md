# Aptitude + OA

**7 sessions. ~75 min each. 18 concepts. 8 files. Done in under 3 weeks.**

---

## Read this first — aptitude is not like your other subjects

```
DSA        →  can you SOLVE it?
CS fund.   →  can you RECALL it?
APTITUDE   →  can you do it in 45 SECONDS?
```

You almost certainly *can* solve every question in a TCS NQT paper if I gave you five minutes each. **You get about 50.** So this course is not about understanding — it's about **the formula arriving before you've finished reading the question.**

That changes everything about how you study it:

| Instinct | What actually works |
|---|---|
| Solve 500 problems | Memorise 20 shortcuts, then solve 200 **timed** |
| Derive it properly | Recognise the type, apply the formula, move |
| Finish every question | **Skip fast. Skipping is a scoring skill.** |
| Practise until correct | Practise until **fast** — correct-but-slow still loses marks |

> **Good news, and it's real:** this is the subject where your effort converts fastest. The syllabus is small, fixed, and rewards drilling — which is the thing you're already good at. It just has to be drilling *the right thing*.

---

## Why it matters more than it looks

**This is a filter, not a test.** At TCS, Infosys, Wipro, Accenture and Cognizant, no human reads your resume until you clear it. You can be excellent at DSA and never get to show it.

Two to three weeks here buys you the *chance* to use everything else you've built.

## The OA format you're preparing for

| Section | Typical | What's tested |
|---|---|---|
| **Numerical Ability** | 20–25 Q, ~40 min | arithmetic, DI, speed |
| **Reasoning Ability** | 20–25 Q, ~40 min | series, puzzles, syllogism |
| **Verbal Ability** | 20–25 Q, ~25 min | grammar, RC, vocabulary |
| **Coding** | 2 problems, 45–60 min | easy–medium — your DSA covers this |

Some have sectional cut-offs (you must clear **each** section), many have **no negative marking** — so an unanswered question and a wrong guess score the same. **Never leave a blank.**

---

## The 4 rules

| # | Rule | Why |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Every drill is timed.** No exceptions. | Untimed practice trains the wrong skill entirely. |
| 2 | **Log slow answers, not just wrong ones.** | Correct in 3 minutes is a fail. It goes in `mistakes.md` too. |
| 3 | **No calculator. Ever.** | You won't have one. Mental arithmetic is half the marks. |
| 4 | **Blank page before notes.** | Same as everywhere else. Reading formulas feels productive and isn't. |
