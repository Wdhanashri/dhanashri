# Ledger — Aptitude

**Open first, every session. Find your row. Do it. Close it.**

1. Recall each concept **from memory** — the anchor, then the formula. Only then check `cheatsheet.md`.
2. Mark: 🟢 came instantly · 🟡 came slowly · 🔴 blank.
3. **Anything 🔴 → write it into the next session's row by hand.**

> **🟡 means fail here.** In every other subject a slow recall is acceptable. In aptitude a formula you have to *derive* is a formula you don't have — it costs you the question.
