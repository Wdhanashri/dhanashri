### A reference shape — compare, don't copy

<details>
<summary>Open after you've scored yourself</summary>

```
Enums:      SlotStatus, PaymentType, MachineState(IDLE, SELECTING, PAID, DISPENSING)

@dataclass  Product(code, name, price)
@dataclass  Slot(slot_id, product, quantity)
                is_empty(), dispense()

ABC         PaymentStrategy → pay(amount) -> bool, refund(amount)
              CashPayment(accepts coins, computes change)
              CardPayment(always succeeds, no change)

class       Inventory        → slots; get_slot(), restock(), is_available()
class       VendingMachine   → inventory + payment strategy, INJECTED
                               select(slot_id), insert_payment(amount),
                               dispense(), cancel() -> refund

Exceptions: OutOfStockError, InsufficientPaymentError, InvalidSlotError
```

**The two things most people miss:**
- **`Slot` as its own class.** Putting `quantity` on `Product` breaks the moment the same product sits in two slots. (That's O-04 — data that travels together.)
- **`cancel()`.** Refunding mid-transaction is the edge case interviewers reach for, and almost nobody handles it unprompted.

**Machine state** as an Enum is a nice touch — the full State pattern is beyond a fresher round, but naming the states shows you noticed.
</details>

**Pass:** 9 of 12 checks, and the happy path runs.

## Keeping it

LLD decays faster than DSA because you use it less often. So:

- **Weekly, 10 minutes:** pick 5 concepts you haven't touched, recall cold, mark them.
- **Monthly, 45 minutes:** redesign one problem from scratch — Parking Lot, Splitwise, Vending Machine, rotating.
- **Week before any interview:** all 21 anchors + one timed 45-minute design.
