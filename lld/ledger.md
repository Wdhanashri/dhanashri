# Ledger — LLD

**Open this first, every session. Find your session's row. Do that. Close it.**

1. Recall each concept **from memory** — the anchor, then what it does. Only then check `anchors.md`.
2. Mark it: 🟢 came fast · 🟡 came slowly · 🔴 blank.
3. **Anything 🔴 → write it into the next session's row by hand.** That's the only maths you do.

> Reviews are counted in **sessions**, not days. At 3 sessions a week the spacing lands where it should on its own.
