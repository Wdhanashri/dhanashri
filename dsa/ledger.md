# Ledger

**Open this first, every day. Find today's row. Do it. Close it.**

Three rules, that's all:

1. Recall each concept **from memory first** — the anchor, then what it does. Only then check `anchors.md`.
2. Mark it: 🟢 came fast · 🟡 came slowly · 🔴 blank.
3. **Anything 🔴 → add it to tomorrow's row by hand.** That's the only maths you ever do.

> **Never mark a concept on the day you learn it.** How smooth something feels an hour after reading it tells you nothing about whether it will come back. A new concept **stays 🔴 until a cold open earns it a mark.**
