## ② 🔵 Stretch problems — only if the ledger is clean

*2-D Dynamic Programming* on **neetcode.io/practice**.

| NeetCode | Problem | Ask yourself | Cap |
|---|---|---|---|
| 2D 10 | Burst Balloons | *Which balloon do you pop LAST, not first?* | 35m |
| 2D 11 | Regular Expression Matching | *What does `*` let you do — zero times, or many?* | 35m |

**2D 10 — the whole trick is reversing the question.** Ask which balloon bursts **last** in a range, not first. Then its neighbours are the range's borders, which are fixed — and the two sides become independent subproblems. Thinking "first" gives you a state that keeps changing shape; thinking "last" gives you `dfs(l, r)`.

**2D 11 — two cases for `*`:**
```
use it ZERO times   →  dfs(i, j+2)                     skip the char and the *
use it ONE+ times   →  dfs(i+1, j)   if chars match    consume one, keep the *
```

**Both are genuinely hard and rare at fresher level.** They're here because they're in NC150, not because you'll be asked them. Skip without guilt.

## ④ The DP self-check — 25 min. **Everyone does this part.**

Nine problems. For each, write **only** the `dp` state sentence in English — no code, no recurrence.

```
1.  Coin Change
2.  Word Break
3.  Longest Increasing Subsequence
4.  Unique Paths
5.  Coin Change II
6.  Longest Common Subsequence
7.  Edit Distance
8.  Target Sum
9.  Distinct Subsequences
```

<details><summary>Check</summary>

1. Fewest coins to make amount `a`. 2. Can `s[:i]` be fully segmented? 3. Length of the LIS *ending at* `i`. 4. Paths from `(i,j)` to the end. 5. Number of ways to make `amount` using coins from index `i` onward. 6. Length of the LCS of `s1[i:]` and `s2[j:]`. 7. Min operations to turn `w1[i:]` into `w2[j:]`. 8. Number of ways to reach `target` from index `i` with `total` so far. 9. Number of ways `t[j:]` appears in `s[i:]`.
</details>

**Score: __/9.** Under 7 → that's the thing to fix before the gate, and it's a bigger gap than any single problem. **The state sentence is the transferable skill; the code is just where you practised it.**
