## Scoring

| Result | What happens next |
|---|---|
| **All parts pass** | ✅ Week 1 done. 14 problems, 2 of your 15 algorithms. Start `week-2.md` tomorrow. |
| **One part fails** | Take **only** the failed concepts back to +1 day in the ledger. Re-teach from the anchor. Retest that part in 3 days. Everything else stays passed. |
| **Two or more fail** | Add 2 days: no new material, only the 🔴 and 🟡 concepts plus the Mistake Book. Not a setback — this is the system doing its job. |

**Never redo the whole week.** Rereading the days you already passed is re-exposure with extra steps — the exact habit this course exists to break.
