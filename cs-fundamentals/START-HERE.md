# CS Fundamentals — OS · DBMS · CN

**9 sessions. ~75 min each. 36 concepts. That's the whole subject at fresher level.**

---

## The one thing that makes this different

Everyone memorises definitions. **Interviewers ask why.**

```
they ask:  "What is a context switch?"          ← the warm-up
they mean: "Why does a context switch cost time?"  ← the actual question
```

Every card here leads with the **why**, because the definition is the part you can guess and the why is the part that gets you the offer. If you only ever learn one thing from this course: *always answer the why before they ask it.*

## The session shape

```
⓪  COLD OPEN     10 min   notes closed, from the ledger
①  4 CONCEPTS    30 min   each one: anchor → why → gotcha → ✍️ blank page
②  🔥 RAPID FIRE 15 min   12 questions, 60 seconds each, notes closed
③  🎤 OUT LOUD   10 min   3 answers, timed, recorded
④  LEDGER         2 min   mark honestly
```

---

## The 4 rules

| # | Rule | Why |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Blank page before notes.** Always. | Reading this material feels *especially* productive. It's especially useless. |
| 2 | **Every answer spoken, not just thought.** | This subject is tested verbally, 100% of the time. Silent knowing scores zero. |
| 3 | **Answer the why, unprompted.** | It's the difference between a pass and a "she actually understands this." |
| 4 | **Every miss → `mistakes.md`.** | Untracked misses repeat. |
