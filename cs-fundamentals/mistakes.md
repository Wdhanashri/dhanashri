## Re-test protocol

1. **Don't read the row.** Just the question. Answer out loud.
2. Right → tick. Two consecutive ticks → the row retires.
3. Wrong again → the concept drops to **🔴** in `ledger.md`. A repeat means it was never learned.

## What your dominant tag is telling you

| Tag | The fix — **not** "read it again" |
|---|---|
| `blank` | Genuine gap. Re-teach from the anchor. Normal, and the easiest kind to fix. |
| `vague` | You know it and can't say it. **Fix by speaking, not reading** — 60-second answers, out loud, timed. |
| `mixed-up` | Build a contrast table for the pair (mutex/semaphore, paging/segmentation, TCP/UDP). Confusion lives in the gap between two similar things. |
| `no-why` | The most important one. You're memorising. For each, write "because ___" and drill *that*. |
| `wrong` | Rare. Fix the fact and move on. |

**`vague` will probably dominate.** That is normal for this subject and it is not a knowledge problem — it's a *rehearsal* problem, and the only cure is saying answers out loud until they come out clean.
